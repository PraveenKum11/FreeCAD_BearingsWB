# Linear bushings

Linear ball bushings for smooth rods: **LM8UU** (8 × 15 × 24) is the 3D printer classic.

![LM8UU, LM8LUU, LMF8UU and LMK8UU cut away at Detailed level](images/linear-bushings.png)

- Sizes LM5UU to LM60UU, and the long LM6LUU to LM60LUU.
- `Flanged` with `FlangeShape` **Round** (LMF8UU) or **Square** (LMK8UU), with four
  counterbored bolt holes.
- Plain bushings have their retaining ring grooves.
- **Standard** adds the rubber seals and the plastic ball retainer, with a slot along
  each ball row; **Detailed** adds the balls, peeking out of the slots onto the rod.

Select the end of a rod and the bushing slides onto it; select a hole and it goes in (a flanged
one rests its flange on the face). Find bearing understands `LMK8UU`, `LMF8LUU` or just
`LMK8`.
