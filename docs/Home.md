# Bearings workbench for FreeCAD

Parametric bearings for FreeCAD, made for makers. Click a hole or a shaft and get a
correctly sized bearing that stays attached to it, or design a fully parametric
**3D-printable bearing** with races, a snap-in cage and balls.

![Standard bearings 6204-2RS, 608ZZ, F688ZZ and MR105 cut away, next to a printable 608](images/hero.png)

## What's in it

| | |
|---|---|
| ![](images/closures.png) | **[[Standard bearings]]**: deep groove series 60, 62, 63, 68, 69, MR and inch R, with shields, seals and flanges, sized from the edge you click. |
| ![](images/angular-thrust.png) | **[[Bearing types]]**: angular contact, thrust, self-aligning, cylindrical, tapered and needle roller bearings, AXK thrust needles. |
| ![](images/linear-bushings.png) | **[[Linear bushings]]**: LM8UU and friends, long, round- and square-flanged. |
| ![](images/printable-turntable.gif) | **[[Printable bearings]]**: races, snap-in cage and balls as PartDesign bodies, with a live preview. |
| ![](images/find-bearing.png) | **[[Find bearing]]**: search by name, bore, outside diameter or height. |
| ![](images/bom-panel.png) | **[[Bearing BOM]]**: every bearing in the document, counted, saved as CSV. |

## Quick start

1. [[Install|Installation]] the workbench and pick **Bearings** in the workbench selector.
2. Select the edge of a hole or the end of a shaft.
3. Pick a series from a drop-down on the **Bearings** toolbar. Done: the bearing is sized
   to the edge and stays attached to it.
